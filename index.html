Clinical fitness

Training built around a diagnosis, not despite it.

Our trainers are fitness professionals, not doctors. We do not diagnose or treat. For PCOS, thyroid, diabetes, hypertension, menopause, bone health or post-partum recovery, we build the training around the advice you bring from your physician or physiotherapist — and refer you back when a question belongs to them.

Ask about a programme

PCOS & PCOD

Strength-led plans that support insulin sensitivity and sustainable fat loss, built around your doctor's advice.

Thyroid

Low-impact progressions and sensible cardio dosing for hypothyroid and thyroid-related fatigue.

Weight & inch loss

BMI testing, measurements and a generic diet plan so you can see progress beyond the scale.

Post-partum

Gentle core and pelvic-floor rebuilding before intensity, at your pace.

Menopause & bone health

Resistance training to protect bone density and muscle through the transition.

Joint-friendly

Machine-led and low-impact options for knees, backs and first-time lifters.
